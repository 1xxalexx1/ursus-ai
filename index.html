FAQ

        
Asked at the den entrance.

      

      

        

          Is my data used to train models?
          
No. Zero retention by default: prompts and outputs are never used for training, and you can wipe memory per project at any time. Grizzly plans can run fully inside your own VPC.

        

        

          Which models does Ursus use?
          
Our router picks the cheapest model that passes your evals for each task — across all major providers. You can also bring your own API keys and pin specific models per agent.

        

        

          Can agents merge their own pull requests?
          
Not by default. Agents open PRs and stop. Auto-merge can be enabled per repo, but only for changes that pass your eval thresholds and stay inside the budget you set.

        

        

          What happens if an agent makes a mistake?
          
Every action runs in a sandbox and produces a diff you can inspect or roll back in one click. Overnight runs are capped by budget, so worst case is a small PR you close — not a small incident.

        

        

          Is the free plan actually free?
          
Yes. Cub is free forever with 100 agent runs a month and no credit card. Most side projects never leave it.
